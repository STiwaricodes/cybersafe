LEGAL AWARENESS

Cyber Law Guide

These summaries are educational only. Whether a provision applies depends on the facts and the applicable law.

IT ACT • 66C

Identity Theft

Addresses fraudulent or dishonest use of another person's electronic signature, password or other unique identification feature.

Example: misuse of another person's password or unique digital identifier may be relevant depending on the facts.
IT ACT • 66D

Cheating by Personation

Addresses cheating by personation through a communication device or computer resource.

Example: an online impersonation scheme used to cheat someone may be relevant depending on the facts.
BNS • 318

Cheating

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 contains provisions dealing with cheating and dishonest inducement.

Example: a fraudulent representation that induces a person to part with property may be relevant depending on the facts.
BNS • 319

Cheating by Personation

The BNS contains a specific provision dealing with cheating by personation.

Example: pretending to be another person to obtain a benefit may be relevant depending on the facts.
This section is for educational awareness and is not legal advice.

DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP

Digital Ethics

01

Protect personal information

Share sensitive information only when there is a clear, trusted reason.

02

Respect privacy

Do not expose, misuse or distribute another person's private information.

03

Never share OTPs

Keep OTPs, PINs and passwords private.

04

Verify before forwarding

Check claims before spreading information.

05

Do not impersonate

Do not pretend to be another person or organization.

06

Report suspicious activity

Use appropriate official channels when something looks wrong.

07

Respect intellectual property

Use and share digital content responsibly.

08

Use strong passwords

Use unique passwords for important accounts.

09

Enable MFA

Use multi-factor authentication where available.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

What To Do If You Are Scammed

01

Stay calm

Stop further communication or payment when you identify a suspicious situation.

02

Contact your bank or payment provider

If money was transferred, contact the relevant provider promptly using an official channel.

03

Preserve evidence

Keep screenshots, transaction IDs, phone numbers, emails, URLs and chat messages.

04

Report through an appropriate official channel

For cybercrime reporting in India, use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or 1930 as appropriate.
